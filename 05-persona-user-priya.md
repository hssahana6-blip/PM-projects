## Background story

Priya read voraciously through university — a book a week during holidays, always something on her nightstand. When she started her career three years ago, the habit quietly collapsed. She tells herself she's "too busy," but she spends 90 minutes a day on Instagram and Twitter. The real issue isn't time — it's that reading now competes with content designed to be instantly rewarding, and books feel like a commitment she might not finish. She has a vague but persistent feeling that she's missing something — depth, concentration, the satisfaction of finishing something substantial.

She downloaded Bookly six months ago, used it for four days, and deleted it. She liked the timer but found it anxiety-inducing. She's on StoryGraph but found the onboarding assumed she already knew what she wanted to read.

## Goals

- **Be:** Someone who reads — a person with an intellectual life outside of work
- **Do:** Finish at least one book a month; build a habit that doesn't collapse under pressure
- **Feel:** The quiet satisfaction of making progress on something that takes more than 30 seconds; less guilty about "not reading enough"
