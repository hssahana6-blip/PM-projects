---

## Buying process & barriers

- **How they buy:** Downloads free tier or free trial first. Decides within 7–14 days based on whether the app has changed their behaviour at all. Upgrades to paid if the habit is forming and the premium features are clearly tied to that habit (not just more content). [ASSUMPTION]
- **Selection criteria:** Does it make me read more? (not: does it have the most books / best design). Evidence of others forming habits. Low commitment entry point.
- **Barriers / objections:**
  - "Another app I'll use for a week and forget about" — the primary objection. Overcome by a strong onboarding loop and an early win within session 1. [ASSUMPTION]
  - Scepticism about subscription pricing for a habit tool — compares to free alternatives (Goodreads). Ceiling ~$8–12/month based on Headspace/Calm/Duolingo Plus analogues. [ASSUMPTION]
  - Switching cost: reading history and book lists are sticky — if he's on Goodreads, moving means losing his shelf. Readio must offer a Goodreads import on day one. [ASSUMPTION]

---

## What moves them

- **Triggers:** A period of feeling "intellectually stagnant" — often after a performance review, a new year, or seeing a peer reading; a specific book recommendation from someone he respects. [ASSUMPTION]
- **Information sources:** App Store search ("reading habit", "read more books"); ProductHunt; Reddit r/getdisciplined, r/selfimprovement; word of mouth from peers. [ASSUMPTION]
- **What closes the decision:** A tangible habit signal within the first 3 days — a streak, a "you read 12 minutes today" summary, a finished article. Early wins are the conversion mechanism. [ASSUMPTION]
