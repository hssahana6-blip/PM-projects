Readio

65 million Americans didn't read a single book last year.
Not because they don't want to. Because no product was ever built for them.

The problem
Goodreads, StoryGraph, Bookly — every reading app is built for people who already read. They track books, compare shelves, display genre filters. But for the person who says "I used to be a reader," they're useless. You open the app, feel guilt about the book you're 12% through, and close it again.

The solution
Readio is a reading habit app — not a reading tracker. It starts with a single question: how do you want to feel? Sets a 10-minute daily goal. Credits every article, newsletter, and chapter equally. And every Monday morning, sends you a Reading Receipt: a warm, honest summary of exactly how much you read this week — and how much more than last week.

Why we win
Duolingo didn't win by building a better textbook. It won by making the first lesson undeniably easy and the progress undeniably visible. Readio does the same for reading. Headspace and Calm proved users pay $70/year for habit formation. We're charging the same — and the segment is ten times larger.

The goal: 200-user closed beta, D7 retention ≥ 35%. We're building the reading habit the other 65 million people have been waiting for.
