# Win/Loss Interview Guide — Readio

**Company:** Readio  ·  **Feature / Product:** Readio — Reading Habit App
**Author (interviewer — objective, not on the sale):** Sahana  ·  **Date created:** 26 September 2026
**Scales:** Perception 1–6 (1 = Poor, 6 = Excellent, NA = Not Applicable) · Price/value 1–5 (1 = Lower, 3 = On Par, 5 = Higher)

> ⚠️ **Evidence caveat:** Readio has no paying customers yet — this is a pre-launch product. This guide is designed for use with **evaluators and lapsed users of competing apps** (Goodreads, Bookly, StoryGraph, Readwise). It should be run by someone objective — not the founder. Goal: understand *why people tried competing apps, why they stayed or left, and what would have to be true for them to switch*. Conduct a minimum of 8 interviews before the findings report. All findings rows below are `[ASSUMPTION]` placeholders to be replaced with real interview data.

## Who to interview

Since Readio has no customers yet, adapt the win/loss frame to three surrogate groups:

| Group | Equivalent frame | Recruiting approach |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lapsed users of Goodreads / Bookly / StoryGraph** | "Loss" — they churned from a competitor | App Store 1–2★ reviews; Reddit r/books; Twitter/X complaints |
| **Active users of competing apps** | "Win for competitor" — understand what keeps them there | App Store 5★ reviews; Discord/Reddit community members |
| **People who downloaded a reading app and deleted it** | "Loss for the whole category" — most valuable for Readio | Reddit r/getdisciplined; personal network |

## Section 4 — Onboarding Experience

- Walk me through your first 10 minutes with the app. What happened?

## Section 6 — Decision to Stay or Leave

- What would have made you keep using it? *(open-ended — do not prompt)*
- What were you using before this app? What made you switch to this one?
- Did you look at other apps before choosing this one? Which ones, and why did you choose this one over them?
- How did you rank the apps you considered? *(1st / 2nd / 3rd)*
- **Where was [app] strongest compared to others?**
- **Where was it weakest?**
- Was there a clear moment where you decided to stay / leave?
- What would it have taken to change that outcome?

## Section 7 — Price & Value

- Did you pay for a premium tier? (Y/N)
- How did the price compare to what you'd expect to pay for something that actually helped you read more? *(1 = too expensive, 3 = fair, 5 = underpriced)*
- Was there a feature in another app you wished this one had?
- What price point would feel right for an app that genuinely changed your reading habit? *(open)*

## Section 8 — Conclusion

- How do you feel about [app] now? *(1–6)*
- Would you try it again if it improved? (Y/N + why)
- Would you recommend it to a friend who wants to read more? (Y/N + why)
- If someone built a new reading app from scratch, what's the one thing they must get right?

## Open questions this guide is designed to answer

- [ ] What is the #1 reason people churn from competing reading apps? (Time, content, motivation, habit collapse?)
- [ ] What does a successful first week look like — what keeps someone coming back?
- [ ] Does social pressure (Goodreads leaderboards, friends' shelves) help or hurt retention?
- [ ] What price point converts a lapsed reader into a paying subscriber?
- [ ] Is Goodreads import a genuine switching barrier, or a nice-to-have?
