## Problem

There is a problem in the industry today: millions of adults who used to read — or want to read — have quietly stopped. They are not short of books. They are not short of apps. They are short of a reading habit.

In Priya's own words:

> *"I used to be a reader. I don't know when that stopped being true."*

The problem is not access. Priya has Kindle installed with three books started. She has a Goodreads account she created and never updated. She reads 60–90 minutes of articles and newsletters every single day — but doesn't count it as "real" reading.

Every existing reading app assumes Priya already reads. Goodreads asks her to log books she hasn't finished and displays friends' shelves that make her feel behind. Bookly gives her a timer that feels like another task to fail at. StoryGraph asks her which genre she wants — when the truth is she doesn't know, because she hasn't been inside a book in eight months.

The market has built excellent tools for avid readers. It has built nothing for the person who wants to become one.

**Scale of the problem:** Pew Research (2023) found that 26% of US adults read zero books in the prior year. That is roughly 65 million people in the US alone who are not underserved by the reading app market — they are invisible to it. [ASSUMPTION — willingness-to-pay within this group unvalidated]

## Solution

The ideal solution for Priya is a reading companion that starts where she actually is — not where she wishes she was.

It does not ask her to log books she hasn't started. It does not show her how many books her friends have finished. It does not give her a timer that counts down.

Instead, it:
- Asks how she wants to *feel* when she reads, not what genre she likes
- Tells her she can read for just 10 minutes today — and means it
- Credits every article, newsletter, and book page equally, so she discovers she is already more of a reader than she thought
- Shows her, at the end of every week, that she made progress — and frames that progress warmly, not as a score
- Gives her a specific next chapter and a specific time to read it, so the next session is already decided

The measure of success is not books finished. It is sessions started.

## Product Description

Readio is a reading habit app for people who want to read more but keep stopping. It uses mood-based discovery, time-aware daily goals, and a weekly Reading Receipt to turn scattered reading intentions into a consistent, rewarding daily habit — across books, articles, and newsletters. *(45 words)*

---

## Problem-Oriented Capabilities

1. **Habit ignition** — makes starting the next reading session the path of least resistance, so "I'll read later" stops meaning "never"

2. **Progress that feels real** — measures reading consistency and momentum, not books-per-year, so Priya sees growth even on days when she only reads one chapter

3. **Discovery without commitment anxiety** — recommends what to read next based on mood and available time, not genre, so she never stares at a blank shelf and gives up

4. **Cross-format reading credit** — counts articles, newsletters, and books as part of the same reading life, so Priya discovers she already reads more than she thinks

5. **The Reading Receipt** — delivers a warm, non-judgmental weekly summary that makes Priya feel like a reader, not a person who failed to read enough
